Tehri Dam Reservoir • Garhwal Hills, Uttarakhand

Adventure on the water, quiet nights by the lake

Ride the lake by day and unwind at a small homestay by its shore. Tehri Lake is the vast reservoir of the Tehri Dam on the Bhagirathi river, ringed by green hills.
